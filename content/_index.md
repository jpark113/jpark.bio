I am preparing Ph.D. applications focused on **U.S.–Japan–Korea trilateral relations**, **digital sovereignty and digital security**, and the geopolitical implications of the **Bering Strait and northern shipping routes**. I am especially interested in how changes in these routes could affect U.S.–Russia dynamics and the positions of Korea, Japan, and China.

I graduated from **Korea University's Graduate School of International Studies** with an **M.A. in International Development and Cooperation in August 2025**. I also hold a **B.A. from Waseda University's School of International Liberal Studies**. My academic background brings development policy and multilateral cooperation into my study of international relations and security.

From March to December 2025, I was a **Program Officer at Human Asia**, coordinating nonprofit education programs and internships. Earlier, I was a **Bilingual General Affairs Specialist at Samsung Electronics Home Appliance** in South Carolina, managing vendors, budgets, and support for international leadership.

My background in international development and program coordination informs this research agenda. I am also open to research, policy, and program roles. See my [research interests](/research/) and [CV](/cv/).
